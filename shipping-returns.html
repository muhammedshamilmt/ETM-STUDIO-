Shipping Policy

We stitch the chaos and send it your way. Every order is a piece of our world, handled with the same punk-cute energy we put into our designs.

All items are shipped within 3-5 business days. Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email to follow your package's journey.

Domestic Shipping

Standard delivery takes approximately 3-7 business days depending on your location. Express options are available at checkout for those who can't wait to join the chaos.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide. International orders may take 10-21 business days. Please note that customs fees and taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

Returns & Exchanges

Not feeling the vibe? We want you to love your ETM pieces. If something isn't right, we're here to fix it.

Returns must be initiated within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in their original condition: unworn, unwashed, and with all tags attached.

How to Return

Drop us an email at chaos@etmstudio.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We'll guide you through the process.
