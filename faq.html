FAQ

What is ETM Studio?

We are a "Punk-Cute" design studio stitching the chaos together. We create kits, patterns, and ready-to-stitch pieces for those who want to express their wild side.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can follow your package's journey from our studio to your doorstep.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes, we ship our chaos across the globe. International delivery times vary by location.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of being placed. After that, we've likely already started stitching your order into reality.

Are the kits beginner friendly?

Absolutely. We believe everyone can master the chaos. Our kits come with detailed instructions and all the supplies you need to start your journey.
